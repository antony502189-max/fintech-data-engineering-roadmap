# 081. IAM, least privilege, and separated roles

## Learning outcome

Assign the minimum permissions required by readers, writers, and publishers.

## How it works

Least privilege restricts actions and resources for a particular identity. Ingestion writers need not change reporting views; analysts need not read raw personal fields. Service identities separate workloads and audit trails. Shared administrator credentials obscure accountability and increase impact.

## Fintech example

A daily-cash reader sees currency totals without customer profiles.

## Implementation steps

1. Inventory resources
2. Separate reading, writing, and publication
3. Assign identities
4. Test denied actions.

## Practical assignment

Build an access matrix for ingestion, transformation, finance, and support. Include negative cases.

## Common mistake and its consequences

One broad identity lets an erroneous development job delete production data.

## Verify the result

The matrix includes concrete allowed and denied requests.

## Self-check question

Why does view access not protect fields if base-table access is also granted?
