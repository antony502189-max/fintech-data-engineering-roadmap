# 025. SCD2 and half-open intervals

## Learning outcome

Preserve historical attributes and select the right version at a boundary.

## How it works

SCD2 stores versions with valid_from and valid_to. A join selects valid_from <= event_time < valid_to; NULL closes no upper boundary. Intervals for an entity must not overlap. A late effective change can split an existing interval and require fact restatement.

## Fintech example

A merchant becomes premium on January 2; January 1 reporting remains standard.

## Implementation steps

1. Normalize effective timestamps
2. Insert the change
3. Repair adjacent boundaries
4. Test exact transitions.

## Practical assignment

Run test_history, add earlier and later versions, and inspect tier_at at every boundary.

## Common mistake and its consequences

BETWEEN includes both ends, joining a boundary fact to two versions.

## Verify the result

Any instant matches at most one version; the new version wins at its start.

## Self-check question

How does a late effective change differ from correcting a previously incorrect attribute?
