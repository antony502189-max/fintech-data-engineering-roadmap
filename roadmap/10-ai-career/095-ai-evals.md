# 095. AI evals: correctness, grounding, and abstention

## Learning outcome

Evaluate stable positive and negative cases.

## How it works

Evaluation data must include actual questions, ambiguous requests, and forbidden actions. Verify SQL by fixture results rather than textual similarity. Grounding asks whether evidence supports claims; abstention checks refusal to invent. Report categories separately so averages do not hide critical defects.

## Fintech example

A correct cash answer and an invented KYC retention requirement represent different evaluation outcomes.

## Implementation steps

1. Collect cases
2. Define scoring rules
3. Isolate critical failures
4. Record model and prompt versions.

## Practical assignment

Calculate pass rates and forbidden disclosures for an offline answer set.

## Common mistake and its consequences

One reassuring average conceals a failed restricted-data case.

## Verify the result

Report financial correctness, citation validity, and critical failure counts separately.

## Self-check question

Why can questions generated by the same prompt inflate apparent performance?
