# 065. FX, presentment, and settlement currencies

## Learning outcome

Convert using explicit rates, times, and rounding rules.

## How it works

Presentment currency describes the customer's amount; settlement currency describes clearing. Rates need pair, direction, timestamp, source, and policy version. Calculate exactly before an agreed minor-unit rounding step. This material uses synthetic rates, not live market data.

## Fintech example

Synthetic USD 10000 converts at 0.91 EUR per USD; a separate fee may already be denominated in EUR.

## Implementation steps

1. Identify both currencies
2. Specify rate direction
3. Choose rounding policy
4. Retain source and converted amounts.

## Practical assignment

Implement Decimal USD→EUR and JPY→KWD examples. Check both currency exponents.

## Common mistake and its consequences

Multiplying minor units directly by a rate ignores different scales and can create tenfold or hundredfold errors.

## Verify the result

Preserve source amounts and rounding deltas; synthetic-rate results are reproducible.

## Self-check question

Why is a rate without time and direction insufficient for financial recomputation?
