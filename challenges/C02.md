# C02. Late dimensions and a historical SQL join

**Level:** intermediate to advanced. **Time:** 2–5 hours. [Related material 1](../roadmap/03-modeling/025-scd2.md) · [Related material 2](../roadmap/03-modeling/024-dimensions.md)

## Scenario and contract

Apply ordered `changes` to valid-time customer history, including out-of-order effective dates and exact repeats. Join `facts` to the version valid at each fact's instant, with intervals [from,to). Normalize offsets to UTC. Missing dimensions use tier=UNKNOWN and never remove facts.

Return `facts` sorted by id, each with id,tier,amount,currency, plus `totals` grouped by tier,currency and sorted in that order. Use an actual SQLite temporal LEFT JOIN. Identical effective changes are idempotent; conflicting tiers at the same effective instant return {"status":"conflict"}. Facts have unique IDs and all fields are well formed.

## Hint

A late effective change splits an interval. BETWEEN includes both boundaries and can create fanout.

## Deliverables and review

The temporal join, its query plan, and totals proving that unknown members do not lose money.

Keep the implementation, passing output, one failing counterexample, and a short note explaining the financial or operational consequence. Check the input contract rather than silently extending it. Synthetic fixtures do not establish production-scale or regulatory guarantees.
