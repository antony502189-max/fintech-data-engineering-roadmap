# C04. Dual schema migration without double counting

**Level:** intermediate to advanced. **Time:** 2–5 hours. [Related material 1](../roadmap/04-ingestion/037-schema-evolution.md) · [Related material 2](../practice/E16.md)

## Scenario and contract

Use the C01 output contract and batch semantics. Source rows have exactly version,amount,event_id,payment_id,kind,currency,event_time,received_at. Version 1 uses positive integer minor units; version 2 uses decimal-string major units. Normalize to the lab contract before comparing business identity; schema version and representation are not new monetary events.

Unknown versions, boolean versions, wrong amount types, unsupported currency, excess precision, missing fields, and extra fields quarantine. Preserve original rejected source input as quarantine evidence even though the output reports counts. Two versions of one identical monetary event are a duplicate. A changed normalized amount under the same ID is a conflict and rolls back its whole batch. Reuse your own C01 pipeline or the lab functions.

## Hint

A version change is not permission to multiply cents by 100 or to allocate a second identity.

## Deliverables and review

A migration trace and a compatibility table for old, new, invalid, and conflicting payloads.

Keep the implementation, passing output, one failing counterexample, and a short note explaining the financial or operational consequence. Check the input contract rather than silently extending it. Synthetic fixtures do not establish production-scale or regulatory guarantees.
